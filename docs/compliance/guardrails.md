# Messaging Guardrails

## Purpose

This document protects the brand from risky public claims and keeps affiliate messaging clear, ethical, and reviewable.

## Review Rule

Any public-facing copy that discusses services, outcomes, commissions, or partner terms must be reviewed before launch.

## Affiliate Disclosure Rule

All partner content must clearly disclose the relationship when a link or referral may generate compensation.

Approved simple disclosure examples:

- I may earn a commission if you use my link.
- Affiliate link.
- Sponsored partner content.

Disclosure should appear early and be easy to notice.

## Earnings Language

Allowed:

- Earn a percentage of referred revenue.
- Build income over time.
- Earnings vary based on effort and audience engagement.
- No inventory required.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed income
- Specific earnings promises
- Instant money language
- Any statement implying everyone will succeed

## Service Language

Allowed:

- Consultation-first pathway
- Licensed provider review
- Online access
- Education and support
- Eligibility depends on evaluation

Avoid:

- Guaranteed outcomes
- Specific result promises
- Diagnosis language
- Claims that imply approval without review
- Product claims that have not been reviewed

## AI Guardrails

AI agents should:

1. Answer general questions.
2. Keep responses short and clear.
3. Avoid giving individualized advice.
4. Encourage the user to apply, book, or contact the team when appropriate.
5. Escalate uncertain or sensitive questions to a human.

## GHL Build Rule

Keep GHL focused on marketing, education, affiliate operations, and lead routing. Sensitive customer-specific details should not be collected in standard GHL marketing forms.

## Launch Gate

Do not activate:

- Affiliate support bot
- Voice AI
- Public funnel
- Recruitment emails
- Social posts
- Partner resource templates

until review is complete.
