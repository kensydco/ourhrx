# Naming Conventions

## Purpose

Consistent naming makes GHL easier to search, audit, automate, and hand off.

## Master Pattern

`OHRx | [Category] | [Function] — [Descriptor]`

Examples:

- `OHRx | Funnel | Affiliate Registration Hub`
- `OHRx | Form | Affiliate Application`
- `OHRx | Automation | Affiliate Application — Confirmation & Notify`
- `OHRx | Email | Affiliate Approval — Link Delivery`
- `OHRx | AI | Conversation — Affiliate Concierge`

## Custom Field Naming

Use prefix: `Affiliate -`

Examples:

- `Affiliate - Primary Niche`
- `Affiliate - Audience Size`
- `Affiliate - Primary Platform`
- `Affiliate - Approval Date`
- `Affiliate - Payout Email`
- `Affiliate - Commission Percent`
- `Affiliate - Partner Tier`
- `Affiliate - Referral Count`
- `Affiliate - Lifetime Revenue`

## Workflow Naming

Pattern:

`OHRx | Automation | [Lifecycle Area] — [Trigger or Purpose]`

Examples:

- `OHRx | Automation | Affiliate Application — Confirmation & Notify`
- `OHRx | Automation | Affiliate Approval — Link Delivery`
- `OHRx | Automation | Affiliate Inactivity — 30 Day Check-In`
- `OHRx | Automation | Resource Vault — Access Granted`

## QA Rule

If an asset does not follow naming standards, rename it before launch.
