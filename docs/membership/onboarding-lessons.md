# Partner Onboarding Lessons

## Objective

Define the first training lessons for approved partners inside the GHL resource area.

## Lesson 1: Welcome and First Steps

Goal: help the partner understand what to do first.

Content outline:

- Welcome message
- Save your link
- Read the disclosure guide
- Choose one starter script
- Share with the right audience
- Ask for help if needed

## Lesson 2: How the Program Works

Goal: explain the basic flow.

Content outline:

1. Partner gets approved.
2. Partner receives link.
3. Partner shares approved message.
4. Visitor clicks link.
5. GHL tracks activity where available.
6. Team reviews performance.

## Lesson 3: Disclosure and Trust

Goal: show partners how to communicate clearly.

Content outline:

- Use a clear relationship disclosure.
- Put disclosure near the link.
- Avoid exaggerated promises.
- Keep language honest and simple.

## Lesson 4: Your First 3 Shares

Goal: create early activity.

Suggested actions:

1. Post one story.
2. Send three direct messages to people who already trust you.
3. Share one FAQ-style post.

## Lesson 5: Weekly Rhythm

Goal: create consistency.

Suggested cadence:

- 1 story per week
- 1 educational post per week
- 3 direct conversations per week
- 1 check-in with the resource vault per week

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create five short onboarding lessons for approved partners: welcome and first steps, how the program works, disclosure and trust, your first three shares, and weekly rhythm. Keep each lesson clear, practical, and action-oriented.
```

## QA

- Lessons are short enough for mobile.
- Each lesson includes one action.
- Disclosure guidance appears before promotional examples.
- No final program terms are shown unless approved.
