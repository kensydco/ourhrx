# Affiliate Program Overview

## Purpose

The affiliate program recruits trusted partners who can introduce OurHealth Rx to their audiences and communities.

## Positioning

This is a mission-aligned partner program, not a generic referral link program.

Core idea:

`Earn by helping people discover a trusted resource.`

## Ideal Partners

Primary partner profiles:

- Health and wellness creators
- Fitness coaches
- Lifestyle creators
- Community leaders
- Barbers and salon owners
- Faith-based connectors
- HBCU network members
- Local advocates
- Digital marketers with relevant audiences

## What Partners Receive

- Unique tracking link
- Partner onboarding
- Resource vault access
- Approved promotional copy
- Graphics and templates
- Program updates
- Support through automated messages and AI assistance

## What Partners Do

1. Apply.
2. Get approved.
3. Receive tracking link.
4. Share approved messaging.
5. Follow disclosure rules.
6. Monitor activity and improve outreach.

## Key Differentiator

Trust matters more than follower count. A small, engaged network can outperform a large but disconnected audience.

## Commission Model

Default model:

- Percentage of referred revenue
- Specific percentage must be verified before publication
- Recurring terms must be verified before publication
- Payout method and cadence must be verified before publication

## Program Promise

The program should promise support, clarity, and opportunity.

It should not promise specific earnings, guaranteed outcomes, or automatic approval.

## Operator Notes

- Keep program selective.
- Use approval workflow.
- Track niche and geography.
- Monitor inactivity.
- Send regular content prompts to partners.
- Keep disclosures visible in all swipe copy.
