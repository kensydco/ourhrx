# SMS Library

## Objective

Provide short SMS templates for GHL partner program workflows.

## SMS 1: Application Confirmation

Thanks for applying. We received your application and will follow up after review.

## SMS 2: Pending Information

We need one more detail to finish reviewing your application. Please check your email or reply here.

## SMS 3: Approved

You have been approved. Check your email for your partner link and next steps.

## SMS 4: Resource Access

Your partner resources are ready. Check your email for the vault link and quick-start steps.

## SMS 5: 30-Day Check-In

Quick check-in. Want a few fresh content ideas to restart momentum? Reply YES and we will help.

## SMS 6: Support Follow-Up

We received your request and will follow up soon.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create short SMS templates for a GHL partner program: application confirmation, pending information, approval, resource access, 30-day check-in, and support follow-up. Keep each message brief, clear, and friendly.
```

## QA

- SMS consent rules are followed.
- Messages are short.
- Links are tested before activation.
- No sensitive details are included.
