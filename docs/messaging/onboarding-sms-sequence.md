# Onboarding SMS Sequence

## Objective

Provide short SMS messages for the partner activation window.

## SMS 1: Day 0

Welcome. Your partner link and resource vault are in your email. Start by saving your link.

## SMS 2: Day 1

First action: choose one starter message from the vault and share it with one audience.

## SMS 3: Day 5

Need help choosing what to post? Reply HELP and we will point you to a starter idea.

## SMS 4: Day 10

Quick check-in. Have you made your first share yet? Reply YES or HELP.

## SMS 5: Day 14

Keep going. Review what worked and choose your next simple post from the vault.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create a short SMS activation sequence for approved partners. Include day 0, day 1, day 5, day 10, and day 14. Keep messages brief and helpful.
```

## QA

- SMS only sends when allowed.
- Messages are short.
- Links are tested if included.
