# Email Library

## Objective

Provide draft email templates for the first GHL partner program workflows.

## Email 1: Application Confirmation

Subject: We received your application

Body:

Thanks for applying to the OurHealth Rx partner program.

We review each application carefully. If approved, you will receive next steps and your partner link.

We will follow up soon.

## Email 2: Approved Partner Welcome

Subject: You are approved

Body:

Welcome to the OurHealth Rx partner program.

Your next steps are simple:

1. Review your partner resources.
2. Save your unique link.
3. Start with the approved starter messages.

Your link: {{affiliate_link}}

## Email 3: Pending Information

Subject: One more step

Body:

Thanks for your interest in partnering with OurHealth Rx.

We need one more detail before we can finish reviewing your application:

{{missing_information}}

Reply here or update your information using this link: {{update_link}}

## Email 4: Not Selected / Waitlist

Subject: Update on your application

Body:

Thank you for applying.

We are not moving forward with your application right now, but we appreciate your interest and may reconnect as the program grows.

## Email 5: Resource Access

Subject: Your partner resources are ready

Body:

Your partner resource vault is ready.

Start here:

1. Read the quick-start guide.
2. Copy your approved starter message.
3. Share your unique link with the correct disclosure.

Resource link: {{vault_link}}

## Email 6: 30-Day Check-In

Subject: Need fresh content ideas?

Body:

Checking in with a few easy ways to restart momentum.

Try one of these this week:

1. Share a personal why.
2. Post a simple FAQ.
3. Send your link to three people who asked about support recently.

Need help? Reply here.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create short GHL email templates for a partner program: application confirmation, approval, pending information, not selected, resource access, and 30-day check-in. Tone: warm, clear, professional, action-oriented. Avoid hype and final term promises.
```

## QA

- Merge fields exist.
- Links work.
- Templates use approved brand voice.
- Review-only terms are not included.
