# Onboarding Email Sequence

## Objective

Provide the first partner onboarding email sequence for GHL.

## Email 1: Welcome

Subject: Your first steps

Body:

Welcome. Start by saving your link, opening the resource vault, and choosing one starter message.

Link: {{partner_link}}

Vault: {{vault_link}}

## Email 2: How It Works

Subject: How the program works

Body:

The flow is simple. Use your assigned link, share approved messages, and keep your communication clear.

## Email 3: Trust First

Subject: Keep trust first

Body:

When you share your link, include a simple disclosure. Clear communication protects your audience and your credibility.

## Email 4: First Share

Subject: Make your first share simple

Body:

Choose one starter caption from the vault and share it with one audience. Do not overthink it.

## Email 5: Weekly Rhythm

Subject: Your simple weekly rhythm

Body:

Try one story, one educational post, and three conversations each week.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create a five-email onboarding sequence for approved partners. Emails: welcome, how it works, trust first, first share, and weekly rhythm. Keep each email short, clear, and action-oriented.
```

## QA

- Merge fields work.
- Links are tested.
- Sequence starts only for approved contacts.
