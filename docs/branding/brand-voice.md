# Brand Voice

## Short GHL Brand Voice

OurHealth Rx writes in a conversational, bold, and trustworthy tone. Keep language simple, clear, and relatable. Focus on access, education, community, and partner opportunity. Avoid hype, jargon, guarantees, or pressure. Sound like someone people trust sharing a real opportunity. Always be authentic, empowering, and action-oriented.

## Voice Attributes

- Bold
- Real
- Empowering
- Culturally relevant
- Approachable
- Trust-first
- Clear
- Human

## Writing Rules

1. Use short sentences.
2. Use plain language.
3. Speak directly to the reader.
4. Lead with mission and credibility.
5. Mention partner opportunity without promises.
6. Make the next step obvious.
7. Avoid generic corporate language.
8. Avoid exaggerated claims.

## Preferred Phrases

- Earn while making a difference.
- Help your community access trusted resources.
- Simple to start.
- Built for real communities.
- Your voice matters.
- Share something that actually helps.

## Avoid

- Guaranteed outcomes
- Instant results
- Get rich quick
- Miracle language
- Pushy urgency
- Overly technical language
- Forced slang

## CTA Style

Use clear calls to action:

- Apply to Become an Affiliate
- See How It Works
- Get Started
- Join the Partner Program
- Access Your Partner Resources

## Example Rewrite

Weak:

Become an affiliate and monetize your audience today.

Better:

Earn by sharing a trusted resource with people who already look to you for guidance.

## GHL Usage

Use this voice in:

- Funnel copy
- Email templates
- SMS templates
- Conversation AI
- Voice AI
- Social Planner posts
- Affiliate resource materials
