# Affiliate Registration Hub

## Purpose

The Affiliate Registration Hub is the primary conversion path for new partner applications.

Suggested URL:

`ourhrx.com/apply`

## Funnel Pages

1. Landing page
2. Application page
3. Thank-you page

## Landing Page Objective

Convert qualified visitors into applicants.

## Landing Page Sections

### 1. Hero

Goal:

- Communicate the opportunity quickly.

Suggested headline:

`Get Paid Helping Your Community Discover Trusted Care`

Suggested subheadline:

`Join the OurHealth Rx partner program and earn a percentage of referred revenue by sharing a mission-driven telehealth brand with people who already trust your voice.`

Primary CTA:

`Apply to Become an Affiliate`

Secondary CTA:

`See How It Works`

### 2. Why OurHealth Rx

Explain:

- Mission-driven brand
- Community-centered approach
- Clear partner opportunity
- Simple link-based model

### 3. How It Works

Steps:

1. Apply
2. Get approved
3. Receive your tracking link
4. Share and earn

### 4. Who This Is For

Cards:

- Creators
- Coaches
- Community leaders
- Local advocates
- Digital marketers
- Everyday connectors

### 5. Earning Opportunity

Use careful language.

Include:

- Percentage of referred revenue
- Potential ongoing revenue share if verified
- Earnings vary by effort and engagement

### 6. Why Now

Explain early partner advantage and growing demand for convenient online care access without making outcome claims.

### 7. FAQ

Include:

- How do I join?
- Is there a cost?
- Do I need a big audience?
- How do I get my link?
- How do payouts work?
- What can I post?
- What support do I receive?
- What disclosures are required?

### 8. Final CTA

Headline:

`Ready to Turn Trust Into Impact?`

CTA:

`Apply Now`

## Application Form Fields

Required:

- First name
- Last name
- Email
- Phone
- Instagram handle
- TikTok handle
- Facebook page or profile
- Audience size
- Primary niche
- City/state
- Why do you want to join?
- How do you plan to promote?
- Payout email
- Disclosure agreement checkbox

Optional:

- Website
- YouTube
- LinkedIn
- Referral source

## Thank-You Page

Message:

`Your application has been received. We review each partner carefully and will follow up with next steps if approved.`

Include:

- Expected review window
- Reminder to check email/SMS
- Social links

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create a 3-page affiliate recruitment funnel for OurHealth Rx.

Pages: landing page, application page, thank-you page.
Tone: conversational, bold, trustworthy, community-centered.
Goal: recruit partners who can refer customers using a unique tracking link.

Landing sections: hero, why us, how it works, who it is for, earning opportunity, why now, FAQ, final CTA.
CTA: Apply to Become an Affiliate.
Application form: contact info, social handles, audience size, niche, city/state, motivation, promotion plan, payout email, disclosure agreement.
Thank-you page: confirm application received and explain that review is required before approval.
Avoid hype, guarantees, or exaggerated claims.
```

## QA Checklist

- CTA buttons work.
- Form submits successfully.
- Confirmation workflow fires.
- Owner notification is received.
- Mobile layout is clean.
- No unsupported claims.
- Disclosure requirement is visible.
- Page title and meta description are set.
