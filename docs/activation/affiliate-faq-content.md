# Partner FAQ Content

## Objective

Create approved FAQ content for partner use in posts, stories, and the resource vault.

## FAQ 1: Do I need a large audience?

No. A trusted personal network can be valuable even without a large audience.

## FAQ 2: What do I share?

Start with approved captions, FAQs, and simple personal context from the resource vault.

## FAQ 3: When should I start sharing?

Start after approval and after receiving your assigned link.

## FAQ 4: What should I avoid?

Avoid making promises, exaggerating, or posting without disclosure.

## FAQ 5: Where do I get help?

Use the support request form or reply to the latest partner email.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create five simple FAQ answers for approved partners. Topics: audience size, what to share, when to start, what to avoid, and where to get help. Keep answers short and safe for social use.
```

## QA

- Answers are short.
- Answers do not include unapproved terms.
- Answers direct users to approved resources.
