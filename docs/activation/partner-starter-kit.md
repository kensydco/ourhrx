# Partner Starter Kit

## Objective

Give approved partners a simple launch kit they can use immediately.

## Contents

1. Quick-start checklist
2. Disclosure examples
3. Starter captions
4. DM scripts
5. Story prompts
6. FAQ post template
7. Weekly rhythm
8. Support request link

## Quick-Start Checklist

- Save your link.
- Read disclosure guidance.
- Pick one caption.
- Share with one audience.
- Track questions.
- Ask support for help when needed.

## Disclosure Examples

- Partner link.
- I may receive credit if you use my link.
- I may earn from referrals through this link.

## Starter Captions

Caption 1:

I partnered with OurHealth Rx because I believe people should have easier access to trusted resources and clear support. Learn more here: {{partner_link}}

Caption 2:

Sometimes the hardest part is knowing where to start. OurHealth Rx makes it easier to learn about options and take the next step online. Start here: {{partner_link}}

Caption 3:

If you have been looking for a simpler way to explore support from home, this may be worth checking out. Learn more here: {{partner_link}}

## DM Scripts

DM 1:

I thought of you because you mentioned wanting a simpler way to explore support options. I am partnering with OurHealth Rx and can send you my link if you want to check it out.

DM 2:

I am sharing a resource that may be helpful. No pressure, but if you want to learn more, here is my link: {{partner_link}}

DM 3:

I joined the OurHealth Rx partner program because I like that it is simple to share and built around access. If you want the link, here it is: {{partner_link}}

## Story Prompt Ideas

- Why I joined
- What makes this resource worth sharing
- A simple FAQ

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create a partner starter kit with a quick-start checklist, disclosure examples, three captions, three DM scripts, three story prompts, one FAQ post template, and one weekly rhythm. Keep everything simple and clear. Use placeholders for the partner link.
```

## QA

- Examples include disclosure language.
- Link placeholders are clear.
- Unsupported claims are not included.
- Copy fits social and mobile use.
