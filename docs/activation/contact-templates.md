# Contact Templates

## Objective

Provide short outreach templates for approved partners.

## Rules

- Keep language simple.
- Ask before sending extra information.
- Include disclosure when a link is shared.
- Keep the tone conversational.

## Template 1

I am partnering with OurHealth Rx and thought it may be useful to you or someone you know. Want me to send the link?

## Template 2

Here is the link: {{partner_link}}

Disclosure: This is my partner link.

## Template 3

Good question. I would start by reading the page and seeing if it feels like the right next step. Here is my link: {{partner_link}}

Disclosure: Partner link.

## Template 4

No pressure. I just wanted to make sure you had the link in case you wanted to look later: {{partner_link}}

## Template 5

I am sharing this with a few people in my circle. If you want to check it out, here is my link: {{partner_link}}

Disclosure: Partner link.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create five short outreach templates for approved partners. Use partner link placeholders and simple disclosure language.
```

## QA

- Templates are short.
- Disclosure appears when link is shared.
- Tone is respectful.
