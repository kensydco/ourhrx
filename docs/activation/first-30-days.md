# First 30 Days Plan

## Objective

Give new partners a simple first-month plan.

## Week 1: Launch

Actions:

- Save link.
- Complete lessons.
- Share one starter post.
- Send three direct messages.
- Ask support one question if needed.

## Week 2: Educate

Actions:

- Share one FAQ post.
- Share one story.
- Respond to questions.
- Review starter kit.

## Week 3: Rhythm

Actions:

- Repeat one story.
- Post one educational topic.
- Start three direct conversations.
- Save common questions for future content.

## Week 4: Review

Actions:

- Review clicks or responses if available.
- Identify best-performing message.
- Choose next month content rhythm.
- Ask for support if no activity happened.

## Ask AI Prompt

```text
Create a first 30 days plan for approved partners. Break it into week 1 launch, week 2 educate, week 3 build rhythm, and week 4 review. Each week should include simple actions and no complex setup.
```

## QA

- Plan is simple enough for beginners.
- Actions do not require advanced tools.
- Support path is clear.
